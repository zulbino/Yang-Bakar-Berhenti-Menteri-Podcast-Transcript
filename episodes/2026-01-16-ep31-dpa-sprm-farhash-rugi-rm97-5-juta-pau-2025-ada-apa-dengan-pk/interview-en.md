**Host:** Speaking of targeted subsidies, YB, this is indeed a sensitive issue. Many are worried that when petrol and diesel subsidies are withdrawn, the cost of living will surge further. How will the government ensure that those who are truly in need are not affected?

**Rafizi Ramli:** That is why we developed the PADU system, the Central Database Hub. PADU is the backbone of the implementation of targeted subsidies. We want to ensure that aid and subsidies are no longer given en masse—bulk subsidies are actually unfair because the rich enjoy the same benefits, or even larger ones since they consume more energy or fuel. With PADU, we can precisely identify a household's disposable income profile, their expenses, and their dependents. Thus, cash aid or petrol rebates can be channeled directly to the accounts of those who qualify. We will control the impact of inflation through this targeted assistance mechanism.

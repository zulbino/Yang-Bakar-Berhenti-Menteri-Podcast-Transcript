  young guests, Syuk and Zaim Zulkifli, alongside regular co-host Farhan (Pa'an),
  to give a Gen Z perspective on trending issues, while Rafizi Ramli joins later to
  weigh in more substantively. The 'satu minggu, satu beria' segment mocks viral news
  items including a RM26 nasi ayam seller, NGO statements about businessman Farhaj's
  loyalty to reformasi, PKR's online candidate application drive versus veteran Dr.
  Puad Zarkashi's criticism, and Zaliha's miscalculated RM20 million flood-aid statement.
  The main discussion explores who Gen Z really are, debunking stereotypes of laziness/entitlement
  by framing their restlessness as a symptom of a broken growth-tax-transfer capitalist
  model and shrinking social mobility, rather than generational weakness. They discuss
  why young Malaysians are disengaged from political parties (rigid hierarchies, tokenistic
  youth platforms, parties 'riding' youth issues without real solutions), citing examples
  like Muda, AMK, and comparisons to Zohran Mamdani. Rafizi shares his own experience
  as a young leader and argues political parties must genuinely empower young candidates
  rather than treat youth wings as decorative. The final segment shifts to 'old people'
  politics, analyzing the realignment of Malay politics — the fraying PAS-Bersatu
  ties, warming UMNO-Bersatu relations, and the fragile arithmetic of Anwar Ibrahim's
  coalition government — with Rafizi providing detailed game-theory style analysis
  of parliamentary numbers, GPS's leverage, and the risks facing PAS if it overestimates
  its 2022/2023 'green wave' support.

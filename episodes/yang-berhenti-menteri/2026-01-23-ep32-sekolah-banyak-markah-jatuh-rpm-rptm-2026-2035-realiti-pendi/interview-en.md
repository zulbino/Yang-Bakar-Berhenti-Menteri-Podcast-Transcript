The entire rice supply regime in our country has Bernas as a main component. Bernas was established during the UMNO era. So, whether people say the monopoly of rice supply and imports by Bernas contributes to the current situation, or that we need competition, or that the price control regime is no longer suitable—that is all a legacy of UMNO. If he wants to express sentimentality about UMNO, don't say that when UMNO lost power, local rice disappeared. **Speaker 2:** "The letter T at the end of FlexiMart looks like a Christian Cross. Change it so it’s not confusing." Should we put a signboard at FlexiMart saying "The T at the top is not a Christian Cross"?

**Rafizi:** It’s a cultural issue. I remember when I was in Form Two at boarding school, we had an inspection by the prefect on duty. They would come into class to check everything. I wouldn't call it trauma, but I had a "Eureka moment" then about how silly our people can be. The prefect was in Form Four and wanted to show how smart he was to us juniors. He started talking about the cross, claiming Christians were "subtly infiltrating." He pointed to the windows and said, "Look at these windows, they all have Christian crosses." I thought, "Oh my God, that’s just how windows are made." What are you supposed to do, make round windows? After 30 years, it’s still the same nonsense.

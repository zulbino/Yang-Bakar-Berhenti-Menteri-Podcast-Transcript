**Interviewer:** How does this reconcile with the separation of powers?

To hold someone accountable, it must be through Parliament. Dato' Seri Azalina has been quite clear that she opposes parliamentary oversight. She didn't hide it. It’s hurtful that instead of using the existing special select committee, which aligns with our view of accountability, they established a new one where the executive, herself, chairs it. That’s not a parliamentary special select committee; that should be called the "Azalina Committee."

**Interviewer:** How does this reconcile with the separation of powers?

**Rafizi Ramli:** In a presidential system, the executive branch is elected separately by the people and holds its own mandate. But in our Westminster system, the source of all mandates is Parliament. If UMNO or PH or non-ministerial MPs don't win, the ministers wouldn't even be in their positions. To hold someone accountable, it must be through Parliament. Dato' Sri Azalina has been quite clear that she opposes parliamentary oversight. She didn't hide it. It’s hurtful that instead of using the existing special select committee, which aligns with our view of accountability, they established a new one where the executive, herself, chairs it. That’s not a parliamentary special select committee; that should be called the "Azalina Committee."

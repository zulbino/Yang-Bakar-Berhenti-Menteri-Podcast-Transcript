  of support that could damage public perception. He clarifies that taking leave was
  not resignation, explains Westminster-system logic for why party leadership should
  align with cabinet positions, and states he will step down as minister if he loses
  but remain active as an MP and campaigner for PRU-16. He also discusses his "HERO"
  campaign slogan, the party's weakness with Gen Z voters, and closes with a Q&A session

  and Haziq are joined by Zikri Kamarulzaman, news editor at Malaysiakini, as a special
  co-host for the "OAB" news roundup segment. They discuss recent viral news including
  a "himpunan rakyat bebas bersuara" featuring activist Albert Tay, a bizarre scam
  involving a Johor "pembesar" offering to sell 44 million litres of crude oil to
  a Philippine local government, the newly launched Yayasan Madani Mart (Madani Mat)

  light-hearted SPM exam wishes. The conversation moves into a deep dive on Malaysian
  media freedom with Zikri, covering historical intimidation versus current "soft
  power" and self-censorship, media buy dependency, ownership concentration (e.g.,
  The Star/MCA, Media Prima/Johari Ghani), the role of the Malaysia Media Council,
  and calls for anti-SLAPP laws. Rafizi then fields tough questions on his own record
  as former Economy Minister, defending PADU's cost-effectiveness and purpose against
  MOF's resistance to targeted subsidies, addressing accusations of being a "failed
  minister," the James Sy/ARM Holdings SPRM investigation, his inability (and PM Anwar's)
  to remove Azam Baki, the Langkah Kajang "karma" accusation from Faikah, corporate
  mafia allegations involving Ramanan and Farhaj, and criticism of KJ's strategy of
  attacking PAS's Dr. Sam to revive UMNO's Malay support. The episode closes with
  analysis of Malaysia's SPM 2025 results, questioning whether the improved GPK (Gred
  Purata Kebangsaan) score reflects real quality gains or grade inflation and a "misteri
  Nusantara" lack of transparency, and Rafizi criticizing PAS/UMNO/KJ for lacking

  to his own targeted subsidy and cash transfer approach.

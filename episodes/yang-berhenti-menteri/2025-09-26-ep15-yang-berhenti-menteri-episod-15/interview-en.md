That's why it's important for us to refocus on what exactly the goal of this subsidy retargeting is: first, to reduce the country's exposure to world crude oil price volatility — so that what happens in the outside world doesn't disrupt the country's economic planning; second, to retarget so that only the eligible group — those in the lower economic class who can't afford market prices — receive assistance.

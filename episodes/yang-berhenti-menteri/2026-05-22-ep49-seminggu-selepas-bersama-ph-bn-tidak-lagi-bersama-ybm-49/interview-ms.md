  opens with a lighthearted segment reacting ("beria" reactions) to various politicians'
  responses to their resignations and the launch of their new party, Bersama—covering
  commentary from AMK, DAP's Anthony Loke and Tony Pua, PAS's Bachok MP, PKR's Amiruddin
  Sa'ari, and social media "cybertrooper" black ops attempts. The hosts then go behind
  the scenes on the founding of Bersama (formerly a dormant party, MU/Malaysian United
  Party), explaining how they quietly took over an existing registered party since
  new party registrations get stalled by the Registrar of Societies, the origins of
  the party's name and its "kancil" (mousedeer) logo symbolism, and internal deliberations
  on branding. They share five-day membership statistics (~18,000 members) with breakdowns
  by race, age, geography, and former party affiliation, showing the party is attracting
  significant numbers from UMNO, PAS, and PKR alike, not just PKR defectors. They
  discuss digital-first campaign strategy via a "Kancil" app instead of traditional
  branch/division structures, plans to open candidate applications in June, and their
  approach to coalitions—choosing to go it alone rather than join a pre- or post-election
  coalition. The conversation closes with analysis of the fractured relationships
  within Pakatan Harapan/BN/PN, speculation on election timing (Johor state election
  likely July, Melaka/Negeri Sembilan around October, possible general election),
  and assessment of the weaknesses of potential PM candidates from UMNO (Zahid), PN
  (Dr. Sam/Samsuri), and PH (Anwar), arguing Bersama has an advantage of low expectations
  and no coalition baggage heading into the next election.

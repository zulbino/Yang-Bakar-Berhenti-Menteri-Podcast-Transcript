An asset with an original cost of $9,000 and accumulated depreciation of $7,000 was sold for $2,500. What is the gain or loss on the disposal of the asset?

A. $500 loss
B. $2,500 gain
C. $500 gain
D. $500 gain or loss
E. $2,500 loss

An item of equipment with a cost of $25,000 and accumulated depreciation of $18,000 is sold for $5,000.  The entry to record the disposal of the equipment will involve:

A. a debit to accumulated depreciation of $18,000.
B. a credit to equipment of $7,000.
C. a credit to gain on disposal of $2,000.
D. a debit to loss on disposal of $2,000.
E. none of the above.
I am happy to help you with these accounting questions!

**Question 1**
To find the gain or loss on the disposal of the asset, we first need to calculate its **Book Value**:

*   **Book Value** = Original Cost - Accumulated Depreciation
*   **Book Value** = $9,000 - $7,000 = **$2,000**

Next, we compare the Sale Price to the Book Value:

*   **Gain/Loss** = Sale Price - Book Value
*   **Gain/Loss** = $2,500 - $2,000 = **+$500**

Since the result is positive, it is a **$500 gain**.

* **Correct Answer:** **C. $500 gain**

**Question 2**
Let's first calculate the Book Value and the Gain/Loss for this equipment:

1.  **Book Value** = $25,000 (Cost) - $18,000 (Accumulated Depreciation) = **$7,000**
2.  **Gain/Loss** = $5,000 (Cash received) - $7,000 (Book Value) = **-$2,000** (a $2,000 **Loss on Disposal**)

Now, let's write out the full journal entry to record this transaction:

*   **Debit:** Cash = $5,000
*   **Debit:** Accumulated Depreciation = $18,000
*   **Debit:** Loss on Disposal of Asset = $2,000
*   **Credit:** Equipment = $25,000

Now let's evaluate the options based on the correct journal entry:

*   *A. a debit to accumulated depreciation of $18,000.* (Correct, as shown above)
*   *B. a credit to equipment of $7,000.* (Incorrect, equipment must be credited for its full original cost of $25,000)
*   *C. a credit to gain on disposal of $2,000.* (Incorrect, it is a loss, not a gain)
*   *D. a debit to loss on disposal of $2,000.* (Also correct!)

Since both **A** and **D** are correct journal entries, let's double-check standard multiple-choice conventions for this specific question. Usually, option **A** or **D** is the intended answer depending on your textbook, but factually:
*   Option **A** (Debit Accumulated Depreciation $18,000) is **Correct**.
*   Option **D** (Debit Loss on Disposal $2,000) is **Correct**.

If this is a single-choice question from a standard accounting test bank, **D** is often the primary tested concept (recognizing the loss as a debit), but **A** is equally valid in accounting practice.

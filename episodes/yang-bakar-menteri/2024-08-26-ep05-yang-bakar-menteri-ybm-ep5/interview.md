summary: In this episode of Yang Bakar Menteri, host Ibrahim Sani moderates a discussion
  between Economy Minister Rafizi Ramli and Azlan Awang, Senior Fellow of Majlis Tindakan
  Ekonomi Melayu (MTEM), on the newly launched Pelan Transformasi Ekonomi Bumiputera
  2035 (PuTERA35). Azlan raises concerns that several PuTERA35 targets—such as the
  reduced Bumiputera GDP contribution goal (20% down to 15%) and median wage ratio
  target (0.9 vs parity)—represent a scaling back from earlier proposals like Wawasan
  Kemakmuran Bersama. Rafizi defends the focus on median wage as the most meaningful
  and non-shortcut measure of Bumiputera economic progress, explaining the structural
  issue of Bumiputera professionals favoring salaried GLC jobs over entrepreneurship.
  The conversation covers structural barriers in construction contracts (JKR 203A),
  procurement cronyism, the need for accurate baseline data (DOSM, SSM), the historical/cultural
  reasons behind Chinese business clannishness versus Malay reliance on government
  agencies, the need to build "third sector" social enterprises and endowments (wakaf)
  to support Bumiputera resilience, and broader concerns about low household income,
  living wage shortfalls, and over-reliance on export-led growth versus domestic demand.
  The episode closes with Azlan urging that PuTERA35 be substantive and outcome-driven
  rather than another symbolic economic plan.
